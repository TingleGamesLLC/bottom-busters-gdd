# The six approved rumors (David, 2026-11-15)

The prototype is built around these six. The game's copy is `proto\rm\hunts.py`.

1. You've mailed yourself a perfumed love letter signed "a secret admirer" every day for a month, and Mildred has to hand them to you with a straight face.
2. You've cried at every children's puppet show this month, the theater seats you behind the kids now, and you bring your own hankie.
3. You got locked out in your nightshirt on Wednesday morning, climbed in through your own kitchen window, and got stuck halfway.
4. You mailed your teddy bear to a toy mender to get its ear sewn back on, with a note so it wouldn't be scared. **Truth:** it's Gary's bear, "Admiral Snuggles."
5. You practice proposing to Bev on one knee every night, to a coat rack in a curly wig, and you've worn a hole in your carpet where you kneel. **Truth:** it's Ozzie, with a cue card in his handwriting that says "my little coffee bean."
7. You talk baby talk to the dock cat and call it Mister Snuggle-Whiskers, and now the whole dock calls it that. **Truth:** it's Toby. He picks up ice at the dock every morning for his lemonade, and while it's loaded he coos at the cat. When the dock crew started asking who it was, Bev, his friend, named you to protect him. Proof: the cat's bed behind the icehouse is one of Toby's lemonade crates with a pillow in it, and "Mister Snuggle-Whiskers" is painted on the side in Toby's sign lettering.

## The hunts, as approved (2026-11-15)

The person who tells you says the sentence word for word. You ask who told each person until you reach the starter, look around a place to find the proof, and call out the lie it catches. In 3 and 4 nobody lied, so showing the starter the proof solves it.

**1.** Toby tells you.
- Chain: Mildred starts it ("You've been mailing yourself love letters signed 'a secret admirer.'"), Bev adds that Mildred has to hand them to you with a straight face, Gary makes it every day for a month, Toby adds the perfume.
- Truth: Ozzie mails himself love letters signed "a secret admirer." When Bev asked who keeps getting the pink envelopes, Mildred named you to protect her friend Ozzie.
- Proof: in Mildred's dead-letter tray, an envelope with no address, typed "a s_cr_t admir_r." At the theater, Ozzie's typewriter is missing its "e" key. Mildred claims she hands the letters to you; the envelope catches her.

**2.** Toby tells you.
- Chain: Bev starts it ("You cried at the children's puppet show."), Gary makes it every show this month, Mildred adds that the theater seats you behind the kids now, Toby adds the hankie.
- Truth: nothing happened. Bev made it up because on Tuesday you told her, in front of customers, that her coffee was lukewarm.
- Proof: the stub book at the theater. One puppet show this month, Tuesday morning, eleven tickets sold, all children's half-price. Bev says she saw you there; the stub book says no grown-up was.

**3.** Mildred tells you.
- Chain: Gary starts it ("You got locked out in your nightshirt on Wednesday morning."), Toby adds the kitchen window, Mildred adds that you got stuck halfway.
- Truth: Bev got locked out in her nightshirt at 6:15 Wednesday morning. From the dock, Gary saw someone in a nightshirt by the coffee shop through the fog and took it for you.
- Proof: still taped to the coffee shop door: "Locked out. Coffee at 7. —B." Gary remembers it was a quarter past six by the coffee shop, which sends you there.

**4.** Bev tells you.
- Chain: Mildred starts it ("You mailed your teddy bear to a toy mender."), Toby adds getting its ear sewn back on, Bev adds the note so it wouldn't be scared.
- Truth: it's Gary's bear, "Admiral Snuggles." It came back from the toy mender with a rain-smudged label, and Mildred read your name on it.
- Proof: the parcel is still on the shelf at the mail office. Inside, the receipt: "One bear, 'Admiral Snuggles,' left ear reattached. Paid: G. Gusto."

**5.** Mildred tells you.
- Chain: Ozzie starts it ("You practice proposing to Bev on one knee, to a coat rack."), Gary makes it every night, Toby adds the curly wig, Mildred adds the hole in your carpet.
- Truth: Ozzie practices proposing to Bev every night, on one knee, to the theater's coat rack. When Gary heard "Bev, my darling" through the stage door and asked, Ozzie said it was you.
- Proof: a cue card in the coat rack's hat at the theater, in Ozzie's handwriting: "Bev, my darling, my little coffee bean—". Ozzie says he saw you through your window; the card catches him.

**7.** Gary tells you.
- Chain: Bev starts it ("You talk baby talk to the dock cat."), Mildred adds the name, which she's seen painted at the dock, Gary makes it the whole dock.
- Truth: it's Toby, as approved, with one change: Mildred is the one who asked, not the dock crew, so the chain doesn't dead-end with people you can't question.
- Proof: Toby's lemonade crate behind the icehouse, with a pillow in it and "Mister Snuggle-Whiskers" painted on the side in Toby's sign lettering. Bev says she saw you there herself; the crate catches her.
