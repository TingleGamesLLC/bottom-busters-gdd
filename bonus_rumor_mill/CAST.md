# Rumor Mill — The Repertory Cast (12)

DRAFT, 2026-11-10. All 12 are made once (Meshy, the chain proven on Bottom Busters' first character) and reused in every game. Like a theater troupe, the same actors get new roles each game from the Casting Director agent.

Each actor is built around one way of distorting a rumor. That style is their **fingerprint**: hear it in a rumor and you know they passed it on.

The player is not one of the 12. The player builds their own character from the parts kit.

---

## 1. Gary Gusto — The Exaggerator
- **What he does to a rumor:** makes it bigger, more often, more people.
- **Fingerprint:** "every day," "hundreds," "the size of a wagon."
- **Look:** enormous handlebar mustache, tall striped hat, barrel chest, arms always out wide.
- **Signature prop:** fishing rod (every fish story is his).
- **Gesture:** the "THIS big" arm sweep.
- **Babble voice:** booming baritone.
- **Roles he can play:** mayor, fisherman, carnival barker, blacksmith.
- **When he's caught:** whatever happens to him, he tells everyone it was ten times worse.

## 2. Toby Crumb — The Salesman
- **What he does to a rumor:** adds one vivid detail. He never plugs what he sells (David, 2026-11-15).
- **Fingerprint:** none. A cheery salesman's voice, but no prices, specials or "tell your friends."
- **Look:** paper baker's hat, apron dusted white with sugar, huge grin.
- **Signature prop:** a tray of whatever he's selling.
- **Gesture:** finger guns, double thumbs-up.
- **Babble voice:** fast and cheery.
- **Roles he can play:** baker, innkeeper, traveling salesman, mayoral candidate.
- **When he's caught:** the natural suspect for the "it was a marketing stunt" twist.

## 3. Nana Pearl — The Mishearer
- **What she does to a rumor:** swaps a word for one that sounds like it.
- **Fingerprint:** sound-alike swaps.
- **Look:** tiny, huge brass ear trumpet, round glasses, knitting needles.
- **Signature prop:** ear trumpet.
- **Gesture:** cups her ear: "EH?"
- **Babble voice:** high, crackly, LOUD.
- **Roles she can play:** oldest resident, librarian, beekeeper, the mayor's mother.
- **When she's caught:** turns out she's sharper than she looks, and some of her mishearings were on purpose.

## 4. Dez Halloway — The Conspiracy Theorist
- **What he does to a rumor:** swaps the motive for a secret plot.
- **Fingerprint:** "they don't want you to know," "wake up," "follow the sprinkles."
- **Look:** tinfoil hat, long trench coat with stuffed pockets, wild eyes, a corkboard under one arm.
- **Signature prop:** his own red string board, which is always wrong.
- **Gesture:** looks over both shoulders, taps his nose.
- **Babble voice:** fast whispers.
- **Roles he can play:** night watchman, mail carrier, hermit, town clerk.
- **When he's caught:** insists getting caught is proof he was right.

## 5. Bev Fairweather — The Drama Queen
- **What she does to a rumor:** adds an emotional detail.
- **Fingerprint:** "and I nearly DIED," "I can't even," fainting.
- **Look:** feather boa, giant sunglasses, enormous sun hat.
- **Signature prop:** folding fan.
- **Gesture:** back of the hand to the forehead, fake faint.
- **Babble voice:** swooping and theatrical.
- **Roles she can play:** playhouse actress, florist, wedding planner, the mayor's spouse.
- **When she's caught:** faints at the worst moment, including in the middle of her own comeuppance.

## 6. Pip Nettle — The Literal Kid
- **What he does to a rumor:** takes figures of speech literally. "They have sticky fingers" becomes "their fingers are made of glue."
- **Fingerprint:** literal readings, "my mom said."
- **Look:** about 8 years old, a helmet two sizes too big, a huge backpack, one sock up.
- **Signature prop:** slingshot (never fired at anyone).
- **Gesture:** points straight at you and yells.
- **Babble voice:** squeaky.
- **Roles he can play:** paperboy, the mayor's kid, apprentice, rival kid detective.
- **Rail:** never the target of slapstick. If Pip started it, the payoff is gentle: his mom marches him over to apologize in front of everyone, or he has to do your chores.
- **Art note:** kid proportions on the same shared skeleton.

## 7. Reggie Fumbleton — The Tavern Regular
- **What he does to a rumor:** swaps names by accident.
- **Fingerprint:** wrong names; he calls everyone "Gerald."
- **Look:** faded noble coat with missing buttons, crooked feathered cap.
- **Signature prop:** a tankard, always in hand.
- **Gesture:** sways, hiccups, points at the wrong person.
- **Babble voice:** slurred and cheerful.
- **Roles he can play:** fallen nobleman, tavern bard, retired sea captain, town judge (alarming).
- **When he's caught:** he was there for the "it was you, at the tavern last night" twist, and he remembers it wrong.

## 8. Mildred Ashby — The Busybody
- **What she does to a rumor:** merges two rumors into one.
- **Fingerprint:** "AND you know what ELSE."
- **Look:** hair curlers, bathrobe, binoculars around her neck.
- **Signature prop:** garden hose.
- **Gesture:** peers through binoculars, leans over a fence.
- **Babble voice:** rapid and nasal.
- **Roles she can play:** next-door neighbor, postmistress, head of the garden club, church organist.
- **When she's caught:** knows every rumor in town, and is the worst at keeping them apart.

## 9. Ozzie Fink — The Liar
- **What he does to a rumor:** swaps the name on purpose, to frame someone or cover a friend.
- **Fingerprint:** oddly specific alibis, "trust me," "I would never."
- **Look:** slicked hair, thin mustache, pinstripe suit a size too small, too many rings.
- **Signature prop:** a deck of cards.
- **Gesture:** shifty eyes, rubs his hands together.
- **Babble voice:** smooth and oily.
- **Roles he can play:** pawnbroker, mayor's assistant, lawyer, card dealer.
- **When he's caught:** the default red herring. Catching him in his own contradiction is the best "crack" in the game.

## 10. Sgt. Brick Hammond — The Shrinker
- **What he does to a rumor:** shrinks it to avoid paperwork. "Minor incident. Possibly a squirrel."
- **Fingerprint:** "nothing to see here," "possibly a squirrel," "move along."
- **Look:** square jaw, helmet too small, tiny whistle.
- **Signature prop:** a notepad he never writes in.
- **Gesture:** waves people along, blows the whistle.
- **Babble voice:** flat monotone.
- **Roles he can play:** town guard, bridge toll keeper, lifeguard, hall monitor.
- **When he's caught:** the straight man. His shrunken versions often land closer to the truth, which makes them a clue.

## 11. Professor Wendell Dimm — The Forgetful One
- **What he does to a rumor:** drops a detail. "Someone stole a thingamajig from somewhere."
- **Fingerprint:** "whatsit," "thingamajig," "you know, the one with the... thing."
- **Look:** glasses pushed up on his head (he's looking for them), mismatched shoes, sticky notes all over his coat.
- **Signature prop:** sticky notes.
- **Gesture:** pats his pockets, scratches his head.
- **Babble voice:** slow and wandering.
- **Roles he can play:** schoolteacher, inventor, town historian, pharmacist (alarming).
- **When he's caught:** his sticky notes are real evidence. They can't mutate.

## 12. Captain Squawk — The Parrot
- **What he does to a rumor:** repeats only the last few words, loudly, at the worst moment.
- **Fingerprint:** fragments plus a squawk.
- **Look:** scruffy green parrot, eyepatch, tiny captain's hat.
- **Gesture:** wing flap, head bob.
- **Babble voice:** squawks.
- **Roles he can play:** Reggie's parrot, tavern mascot, the town's escaped pet.
- **Rail:** never the target of slapstick. If Squawk started it, the payoff is that you teach him to shout something embarrassing about the next culprit, or he gets a tiny Liar's Hat.
- **Art note:** not humanoid, so not on the shared skeleton. Wing flap and head bob keyframed by Blender script.

## Mutation coverage

| Mutation | Who does it |
|---|---|
| Exaggerate | Gary |
| Shrink | Sgt. Brick |
| Swap the name (by accident) | Reggie |
| Swap the name (on purpose) | Ozzie |
| Swap a word by sound | Nana Pearl |
| Swap the motive | Dez |
| Add a detail | Toby (a vivid detail), Bev (drama) |
| Drop a detail | Prof. Dimm |
| Merge two rumors | Mildred |
| Take it literally | Pip |
| Keep only the end | Captain Squawk |
